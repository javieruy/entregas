Where to start

  1. Open the main book, pick one of the 100 masterpieces and print the page at home (A4 or US Letter paper, “Fit to page”).
  2. Look at the real painting, read the three short stories and color the drawing with the color guide.
  3. Keep going with the 7 days of First Steps and the 30 missions of Detail Hunt.
  4. Once you have a few finished pages, put on a show at home with My First Art Show.
